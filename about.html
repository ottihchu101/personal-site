My Story

TechnologyFintechEconomics

I was born in Dallas, grew up in Nigeria, and wrote my first lines of Python during A-levels in the UK. It didn't click right away, and for a while I wasn't sure it ever would. Sticking with it taught me that I learn best by building something, breaking it, and figuring out why.

That stubbornness turned into genuine curiosity. One language led to another, then to frameworks, design tools, and a growing interest in how good software is put together, not just whether it works.

I currently study Computer Science, with a minor in Economics, at Emory University. I'm drawn to practical, user-centered work: software that solves a real problem and is clear and pleasant to use. I mostly build with JavaScript, Python, and Node.js, and I care about writing code that's clean and easy to maintain. I'm especially interested in how AI is changing the way software gets built, and I try to stay close to that shift.

When I'm not coding, I'm probably planning the next trip, reading about how economies and policy shape people's lives, or playing basketball or soccer.
