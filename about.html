My Story

Aspiring Software EngineerFocused on Impactful Products

Born in Dallas but raised in Nigeria, I spent six years in boarding school before heading to the UK for A-levels. That's where I first tried Python, and it was rough. The basics didn't click right away, and I struggled a lot at the start. But sticking with it changed everything.

That frustration turned into real curiosity, and soon I was digging into more languages, frameworks, design tools, and ways to build things that actually work well.

Now, as a Computer Science major with an Economics minor at Emory University, I'm focused on creating practical, user-centered solutions that solve real problems and deliver clear value. I build with tools like JavaScript, Python, Node.js, and others, always aiming for clean, efficient code that prioritizes outcomes over show. I keep up with what's happening in the field too, especially how AI is changing development, making workflows smarter and more adaptive.

Outside of tech, I love understanding how the world works: how economies grow, how policy shapes people's lives, and how global trends connect. I recharge through basketball, soccer, fashion, culture, and travel. Everything here, from projects to experiences, reflects what's shaped me so far.
